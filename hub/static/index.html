🫔tamale-hub

See all sessions

Your tamale sessions, from anywhere.

tamale-hub puts tamale's web UI on your phone or any browser, wherever tamale runs: your laptop or a machine in the garage. No port forwarding, no VPN. Each tamale connects out to the hub, and your browser holds the key that lets you in.

  1. Make a key below.
  2. On each machine, set it where tamale runs and run /remote in tamale.
  3. On your phone, scan its code. Your sessions show up here; tap one to drive it.

Your key

This key gives access to every machine that uses it: treat it like an SSH key. A machine you trust less, like a throwaway VPS, should get a key of its own.

          How much to trust this hub
          
    Sessions are end-to-end encrypted between tamale and your browser. Your key is made in this
            browser and never reaches this server. Whoever runs the hub, or breaks into it, can't read your
            prompts, code, tool output, artifacts, titles or directories, and can't send commands to your
            machines.
    
          
    Three limits. This server delivers the page code, so someone in control of
            it could change that code to steal a key the next time a page loads, or as one is made;
            encryption makes an attack take that active step, but doesn't rule it out. It still sees
            metadata: which sessions exist and when, from which addresses, whether they're
            busy, and message sizes and timing. And it can't protect a compromised laptop or phone: a
            machine that holds a key can reach every other machine using it, and in tamale's
            auto mode run commands there without approval. Give machines you trust less a key
            of their own. It's a beta: for anything sensitive, run your own hub (it's one small binary
            behind Caddy), and keep tamale's permission mode on ask for remote work.
    
          
    Unused keys made here are deleted after 7 days if they never connect, and after 90 days
            without use.
